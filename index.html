Before you choose

Good questions, honest answers

Can I start with the Duo and add oil later?

Yes. Wash Duo is a complete cleanse and condition. Add Split Ends Care whenever your ends ask for more.

I have fine hair — will the oil weigh me down?

Keep it on the ends only. Start with 1–2 drops. The pre-wash step rinses out before you style.

What’s different in the shower?

Full Routine adds one step before you wash: oil on dry ends. Same shampoo and conditioner either way.

Is this the Curly collection page?

No. You’ll see the same wash bottles there — this page is only for choosing Duo vs Full Routine.
